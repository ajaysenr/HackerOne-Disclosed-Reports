## Program Summary

> A TikTok seller endpoint had a cross-site scripting (XSS) vulnerability via the "__hack_redirect_now__" parameter, caused by the reflection of user-supplied data without proper HTML escaping or output encoding. This vulnerability has been resolved. We thank @7hamoody1 for reporting this to our team.
