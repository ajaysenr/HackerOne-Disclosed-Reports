## Summary:
While Conducting my research I discovered that I'm able to see anyone's `reactions received` on the notification bar of his account.


==for the seek of your valuable time, I think if you watched the POC Video first before reading the report, things will be easier and then you can read the report for the details.==

## Steps To Reproduce:
* 1. Go to `https://try.discourse.org/` and create a new account
* 2. Then go to your summary profile `https://try.discourse.org/u/{Your_Username}/summary` make sure to change `{Your_Username}` to your username, in my case my username is `testBugs` as you see in the below picture, and also observe on the below picture that you will have access to 5 tabs in your profile.

{F2866672}

* 3. Then go to any other user's summary profile, for example go to this user `https://try.discourse.org/u/codinghorror/summary` and observe in the below picture that you will have access to only 3 tabs if it's any other user's profile.

{F2866675}




## So basically from the above pictures you observed that you can't access the notifications bar of any other user on the browser cause there's no notification button to click on.

## So the notification bar itself is indeed accessible to only the user of the account.

My notification looks good and accessible, nice, but what if we change the username on the URL `https://try.discourse.org/u/testbugs/notifications` to `https://try.discourse.org/u/codinghorror/notifications` to access his notification bar?

* 2. Here's the attempt to access the notification bar of  the user whose username is `codinghorror`:

{F2866701}

nice To this point it looks good and secure and the server-side checks with the Tokens properly and restricts access to the notification bar if I'm not the owner of the account.


but while digging deep and deep into the notification bar I discovered that there are multiple endpoints on the notification bar:

{F2866716}

* 1)   /notifications/responses
* 2)   /notifications/likes-received
* 3)   /notifications/mentions
* 4)   /notifications/edits
* 5)   /notifications/reactions-received




let's do the same technique of changing the URL in an attempt to access other user notification functions:

* 1. changing `https://try.discourse.org/u/testbugs/notifications/responses` to `https://try.discourse.org/u/codinghorror/notifications/responses`     gives `403 Forbidden`

* 2. changing `https://try.discourse.org/u/testbugs/notifications/likes-received` to `https://try.discourse.org/u/codinghorror/notifications/likes-received`     gives `403 Forbidden`

* 3. changing `https://try.discourse.org/u/testbugs/notifications/mentions` to `https://try.discourse.org/u/codinghorror/notifications/mentions`     gives `403 Forbidden`

* 4. changing `https://try.discourse.org/u/testbugs/notifications/edits` to `https://try.discourse.org/u/codinghorror/notifications/edits`     gives `403 Forbidden`

* 5. changing `https://try.discourse.org/u/testbugs/notifications/reactions-received` to `https://try.discourse.org/u/codinghorror/notifications/reactions-received`     gives ==200 OK==      :)


so yeah the last endpoint which is `/notifications/reactions-received` is accessible to anyone to see,  just all he needs is to change the URL and he will be able to see any user's reactions-received on their notifications bar.
