## Program Summary

> @uranium238 discovered a vulnerability with a 3rd party email integration provider which would allow messages in Slack email integrations to be leaked. We worked with the 3rd party to get this issue resolved, and performed a thorough investigation to confirm that this had never been exploited. Thanks @uranium238 for an interesting finding!
