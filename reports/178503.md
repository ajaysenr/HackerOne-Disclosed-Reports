## Program Summary

> The `www.uber.com/a/join?invite_code=inviteCode` endpoint returned email and/or phone number for the user with the invite code `inviteCode`. 

Since protecting our users data is our top priority, we were very interested in this report. It was also a pleasure to work with @kushal89shah and we look forward to more reports in the future!
