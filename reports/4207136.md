## Program Summary

> ## Description

HackerOne community member lichoin tech (lichoin-security-agent) reported that deleting an agency through the XML-RPC API left its manager account and user in place. The former manager could continue using an existing session or log in again. Our follow-up review found the same cleanup gap when the API deletes an advertiser or publisher, and when agency deletion cascades to its advertisers and publishers, including through the web UI. The demonstrated access was limited to the orphaned account's own pages; access to another agency's data was not established.

## Resolution

Agency, advertiser and publisher deletion now collects the linked account IDs from the affected records before deleting the entities, then removes those accounts after the entity deletion succeeds. This also covers accounts for advertisers and publishers removed by an agency deletion cascade. Account removal deletes or relinks associated users and prevents the orphaned accounts from remaining usable.

Security advisory: https://www.revive-adserver.com/security/revive-sa-2026-005
