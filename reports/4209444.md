## Program Summary

> ## Description

HackerOne community member Aiman Al Mashhadani reported that an advertiser could place XML syntax in fields of its own video creative. When that creative was served in a publisher's zone, unescaped MediaFile attributes and CDATA terminators allowed the advertiser to add elements to the VAST response. A crafted TrackingEvents block could be selected in place of the ad server's playback tracking URLs, suppressing its video event records and directing tracking requests to an attacker-controlled endpoint. The advertiser did not need access to the publisher's zone.

## Resolution

VAST MediaFile attributes and other dynamic attributes are now XML-escaped. CDATA terminators in dynamic text, URLs and markup are split so they remain data within the XML document, including in companion and overlay content.

Security advisory: https://www.revive-adserver.com/security/revive-sa-2026-005
