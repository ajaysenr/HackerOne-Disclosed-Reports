## Program Summary

> LINE Social Plugins (https://social-plugins.line.me/) is a service that provides LINE users with content sharing on the web.
This Blind SSRF attack was caused by bypassing the DNS verification of the parameter value received. It could have made requests to internal servers or scanned internal network ports.
