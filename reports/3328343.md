## Program Summary

> A missing validation on the customer_bid field when creating rental trips allowed passengers to submit arbitrary bid amounts, including very low fares. While drivers would not have accepted such low-balled offers, the system displayed these manipulated fare trips without verification. Proper validation is now added to prevent unrealistic values.
