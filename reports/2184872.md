## Program Summary

> In this report, the researcher reported an exposure of CDN access token that allows an attacker to modify newly uploaded photo in GTA5 Snapmatics. The access token retained validity for a brief interval subsequent to the upload process, thereby providing a window of opportunity for content modification.
This issue has been resolved by removing CDN fields from the response.
